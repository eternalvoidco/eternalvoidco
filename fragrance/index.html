The Olfactory World

The Scent

Some presences are felt before they are seen. They move through silence. And remain after they have gone.

  1. I Opening

    First Light

    A cold spark breaks the dark. Bergamot and blackcurrant sharpen the air; pink pepper flickers, and saffron draws a thread of gilded heat.

    Notes

    • Bergamot
    • Blackcurrant
    • Saffron
    • Pink pepper

  2. II Heart

    The Inner Chamber

    Behind closed doors, rose and jasmine open in the half-light. Incense rises from smoked woods: slow, devotional, close.

    Notes

    • Rose
    • Jasmine
    • Incense
    • Smoky woods

  3. III Base

    What Remains

    Oud and leather settle into the dark, softened by amber and vanilla. Patchouli and musk hold what is left: a presence, not a trace.

    Notes

    • Oud
    • Amber
    • Vanilla
    • Leather
    • Patchouli
    • Musk
