Fit Guide

Oversized Tee Fit Guide

Choosing an oversized tee is about proportion, not just size up. Use measurements and your preferred silhouette outcome.

Measure these first

  • Chest width (pit to pit)
  • Body length
  • Shoulder width
  • Sleeve length

Simple fit rules

For controlled oversized, choose your regular size in oversized patterns. For deeper drape, go one size up. For cleaner luxury streetwear proportions, go one size down from max-oversized references.

VOID© fit direction

VOID© tees are intentionally oversized. Start from your true size and adjust by style preference for trim, balanced, or deep oversized.
