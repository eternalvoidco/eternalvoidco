Submit an original garment concept, artwork, or tech pack to ETERNAL VOID. Selected designs may be developed into a limited release, with designer credit and a royalty on sales.

Submit a Design Collaboration Terms

01 What you can submit

A sketch is enough to start.

We read original work from people who make things. You do not need a studio, a label, or a production history — you need a design you own and can describe.

  • Garment concepts

    A cut, a silhouette, a construction idea. Flats, a sketch, or a photographed toile.

  • Graphics & artwork

    Front or back placements, all-over compositions, typographic work, drawn or painted originals.

  • Textile prints

    Repeats, colourways, treatments. A clean tile and a mock-up on a body are plenty.

  • Embroidery & tech packs

    Stitch artwork, thread calls, or a full pack if you have one. A full tech pack is optional.

A clear sketch or mock-up and a short description are enough to start. We ask for editable or production source files only once a design progresses — never at intake.

03 What happens next

From your file to a release.

  1. You submit, privately

    Your files go to private storage. They are not published, not indexed, and not attached to any email. You get a confirmation and a submission reference straight away.

  2. The atelier reads it

    A small internal review team looks at the work. Nothing is shown outside that team, and nothing is used for anything other than deciding whether to talk to you.

  3. A conversation, if shortlisted

    If the work is shortlisted we write to you. Shortlisting is a stage of review — it is not an offer and it grants no rights in your design.

  4. A written offer

    If we want to produce it, you receive a written collaboration agreement naming the design, the products, the channels, the credit and the royalty. Nothing is made until both parties sign it.

  5. Development and release

    Sampling, approvals, production, launch. You are credited, and the royalty runs from the first eligible sale.

We do not publish a guaranteed response time. Intake is read in batches by a small team, and we would rather say nothing than promise a date we cannot keep. If a design is shortlisted you will hear from us directly; if you have heard nothing, your submission is still on file and you can ask where it stands at any time.

04 How you are paid

A royalty on what the piece actually sells for.

12%

of net product sales, proposed

Twelve per cent of net product sales is ETERNAL VOID’s proposed opening offer for a selected design. It is our proposal, not an industry benchmark, and the rate in your agreement is the one that counts.

What the royalty is calculated on

For direct sales, the royalty is calculated on the amount actually paid for the collaboration merchandise, after discounts. VAT and other sales taxes are excluded, and so is separately charged shipping.

Refunds and chargebacks reverse the affected merchandise amount once, including partial reversals. A reversal that lands in a later quarter is applied to that quarter.

What is not deducted

Production, sampling, packaging, platform fees, payment fees, marketing and overhead stay outside the royalty calculation. ETERNAL VOID covers these when it runs the release — they are not netted off your royalty.

An example, in plain language

If an eligible sale generates €160 in net product sales, a 12% royalty pays the artist €19.20.

When you are paid

We propose itemised quarterly statements, paid within 30 days of quarter end. Each statement shows eligible units, discounts, reversals, the royalty base, the rate and the amount due.

Development work

If we ask you for a production-ready tech pack, additional artwork, or substantial revisions after selection, we agree a separate development fee and a scope before that work starts. Development fees are paid whether or not the piece reaches release.

Which sales count

The agreement names the SKUs and the channels the royalty applies to. Wholesale, bundles and marketplaces are only used once their terms are set. Restocks, new colourways and any other use are covered in the agreement or by a written amendment.

Proposed terms, pending approval. The rate, the payment terms and the reporting format above are ETERNAL VOID’s proposal and are being reviewed by the owner, by accounting and by Hungarian counsel before intake opens. The signed agreement governs.

05 Credit, ownership, withdrawal

Your work stays yours.

How you are credited

The agreement names the credit you are given: the name you want to be known by and where it appears — the product page and the campaign materials we agree. If you prefer a studio name or a pseudonym, that is the name we use.

Who owns the work

You keep ownership of what you send. The intake terms allow one thing: limited internal review by the people deciding whether to make you an offer. Production, marketing and sale require a separate signed agreement.

What submitting does not grant

Submitting gives ETERNAL VOID no permission to publish your work, manufacture it, use it commercially, or train AI systems on it. There is no submission fee, and there never will be.

What else could come from it

Selected collaborators may be invited to private previews, to ETERNAL VOID events, or to conversations about future capsules. These are possibilities, not terms — the signed commercial terms and the compensation come first.

Withdrawing a submission

Write to artists@eternalvoid.co with your submission reference and ask us to withdraw it. We mark the submission withdrawn, stop reviewing it, and delete the uploaded files. We keep a minimal record that a submission existed and was withdrawn, and the accepted terms version — that record is what protects both of us.

Reporting a rights dispute

If you believe work submitted here is yours and was sent by someone else, write to legal@eternalvoid.co. Tell us what the work is and what your claim is. We suspend review of the submission while we look into it.

07 Questions

Before you send unpublished work.

Does submitting cost anything?

No. There is no submission fee and no fee at any later stage. If anyone asks you to pay to be considered by ETERNAL VOID, it is not us.

Who actually sees my files?

A small internal review team at ETERNAL VOID, and the technical providers that host the storage and send the email. Files sit in private storage and are reached through short-lived authorised links. They are not published, not indexed, not sent as email attachments, and not shared with a manufacturer unless and until you have signed an agreement that names that access.

Can I submit work made with someone else?

Yes, if you have your co-authors’ permission to submit it and to license it, and you tell us who they are. The same applies to any third-party element in the work — a font, a photograph, a sample. If you cannot clear it, leave it out.

What if you make something similar on your own?

ETERNAL VOID designs continuously and independently. Submitting does not stop us from developing our own work, including work that resembles yours. What the intake terms do is keep your submission out of production unless we sign an agreement with you for it.

How long do you keep my files?

Uploaded files are kept while the submission is under review and for a limited retention period after it is closed, then deleted. The exact period is stated in the Submission Privacy Notice. You can ask us to withdraw and delete earlier at any time.

Do I have to be a professional designer?

No. The programme exists for emerging designers and artists. You need to be old enough to enter a contract where you live, and the work needs to be yours.
