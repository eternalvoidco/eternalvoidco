EV for Artists

Submission Terms

Version 2026-11-19 · Draft 1 · Governing language: English

These terms apply when you send a design to ETERNAL VOID through the EV for Artists page. They cover one thing: what happens to your work between you sending it and either of us signing an agreement about it. They are short on purpose.

1 · Who these terms are with

EV for Artists is operated by [LEGAL NAME] Kft., a company registered in Hungary (company registration number [CÉGJEGYZÉKSZÁM], tax number [ADÓSZÁM], registered seat [SEAT ADDRESS]), trading as ETERNAL VOID (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at artists@eternalvoid.co.

2 · Who can submit

  • You must be old enough to enter into a binding contract where you live. If you are under that age, do not submit.
  • You may submit as an individual, a studio or a company. If you submit on behalf of an organisation, you confirm you are allowed to.
  • There is no submission fee, at intake or at any later stage. We will never ask you to pay to be considered.
  • We may decline or close any submission without giving a reason.

3 · What you are telling us when you submit

By submitting, you confirm that:

  • the work is yours and original, or you are authorised to submit and to license it;
  • if the work has co-authors, you have their permission both to submit it and to license it, and you will tell us who they are;
  • any third-party element in the work — a typeface, a photograph, a sample, an AI-generated component — is cleared for the use contemplated here, or you have identified it to us;
  • to the best of your knowledge the work does not infringe anyone else’s rights;
  • the work is not already exclusively licensed or assigned to somebody else.

If any of this stops being true after you submit, tell us at artists@eternalvoid.co.

4 · You keep your work

You retain all ownership and all intellectual property rights in what you submit. Nothing here transfers, assigns or exclusively licenses anything to us. Your moral rights as author are unaffected.

You grant us one limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free, revocable permission, for the sole purpose of deciding whether to approach you about a collaboration: to receive, store, open, view and internally discuss your submission, and to make the internal copies that storage and review technically require.

That permission is all you are granting. Submitting does not give us permission to publish your work, manufacture it, sell it, use it in marketing, sub-license it, or use it to train, fine-tune or evaluate AI or machine-learning systems. Production, marketing and sale require a separate agreement signed by both of us.

5 · Who sees it

Your submission is read by a small internal review team at ETERNAL VOID and by the people who operate the systems that hold it. Files are kept in private storage with no public access and are opened through short-lived authorised links. Your work is not published, not indexed by search engines, not sent as an email attachment, and not shared with a manufacturer or any other third party unless and until you have signed an agreement that names that access.

We treat your submission as confidential and will not disclose it outside that group, except where we are legally required to, or to our professional advisers under a duty of confidence. This obligation is what we can actually deliver with the review and storage arrangement described above; we have deliberately not promised more than that.

6 · Independent development

We design continuously and independently, and we receive submissions from many people. Nothing here restricts us from developing, commissioning or releasing our own designs, including designs that may resemble yours, where they are created independently of your submission. What these terms do is keep your submission out of production unless we sign an agreement with you for it.

7 · If we want to make it

Shortlisting and any submission confirmation are stages of review. They are not an offer, not an acceptance, and they create no commercial relationship and no rights in your design.

If we want to produce a design, we will send you a written collaboration agreement naming the design, the products, the sales channels, the territory, the term, the credit and the royalty. That agreement is the only thing that permits commercial use, and it only does so once both parties have signed it. Compensation terms proposed on the programme page are proposals and are superseded by the signed agreement.

8 · Withdrawing a submission

Write to artists@eternalvoid.co quoting your submission reference and ask us to withdraw it. We will mark the submission withdrawn, stop reviewing it, and delete the uploaded files.

We keep a minimal record that a submission existed, who it was from, when it was made and withdrawn, and which version of these terms you accepted. We keep that because it is the evidence of what each of us agreed to, and it protects both of us. The full retention periods are in the Submission Privacy Notice.

Withdrawal does not undo anything already done under a signed agreement, if one exists.

9 · Reporting a rights dispute

If you believe work submitted here is yours and was submitted by somebody else, write to legal@eternalvoid.co. Tell us what the work is, what your claim is, and how we can reach you. We will suspend review of that submission while we look into it, and we may close it. We do not adjudicate ownership disputes between third parties; where a claim is contested we will decline the submission and leave the parties to resolve it.

10 · No warranty, and limits

The programme is provided as it stands. We do not promise a response, a response time, a shortlisting, an offer, or a release. We are not responsible for the loss or corruption of submitted material beyond keeping it as described above; keep your own copies of everything you send. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or under mandatory Hungarian consumer or author-protection law.

11 · Changes

We may change these terms for future submissions. The version that applies to your submission is the version shown when you submitted it, which we record against your submission and quote in your receipt. We will not apply a later version retroactively.

12 · Law and disputes

These terms are governed by Hungarian law, without prejudice to any mandatory protections available to you under the law of your country of residence. Disputes are subject to the courts of [VENUE — to confirm with counsel], again without prejudice to any mandatory rule that gives you the right to bring proceedings elsewhere.

Open items for review

Not part of the terms. This list is for the owner and for counsel, and is removed before these terms are finalised.

  1. Company facts. Registered legal name, company registration number, tax number, registered seat, and the name of the representative who signs. None of these are recorded anywhere in the project and all are needed in clauses 1 and 12.
  2. Hungarian Copyright Act (1999. évi LXXVI. törvény). Counsel to confirm the limited-review permission in clause 4 is correctly framed as a licence (felhasználási engedély) rather than a transfer, and that it is valid without being in a signed written instrument.
  3. Moral rights. Counsel to confirm that clause 4’s wording is consistent with the inalienable author’s rights under Hungarian law, and that clause 10’s carve-out is sufficient.
  4. Design rights. Whether a Community design or a Hungarian industrial design registration is contemplated, and whether the twelve-month grace period is affected by anything in the intake process.
  5. Independent development (clause 6). Counsel to confirm this is enforceable as drafted and does not undercut the confidentiality undertaking in clause 5.
  6. Venue and minors. Confirm the venue in clause 12, and whether a guardian’s consent mechanism is required rather than the age statement in clause 2.
  7. Localisation. Whether a Hungarian-language version is required, and which language governs if both exist.
