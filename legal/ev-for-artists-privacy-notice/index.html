4 · Who can reach it

  • Our internal review team. A small number of people at ETERNAL VOID. Files sit in private storage with no public access and are opened through authorised links that expire within minutes.
  • Our processors, acting on our instructions under Article 28 agreements: Supabase (database and private file storage — hosting region to confirm), Vercel (application hosting — function region to confirm), and Resend (transactional email).
  • Our professional advisers, under a duty of confidence, where we need advice.
  • Authorities, where we are legally required to disclose.

Your work is not published, not indexed, not sent as an email attachment, and not shared with a manufacturer or any other third party unless and until you have signed an agreement naming that access.

7 · Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your personal data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and you may object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interests. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting processing already carried out.

Write to privacy@eternalvoid.co, or to artists@eternalvoid.co with your submission reference to withdraw a submission. We respond within one month.

Erasure has one limit, and we would rather state it than surprise you: after we delete your files we keep a minimal record that a submission existed, who made it, and which terms they accepted. That record is how either of us can later show what was and was not agreed.

You can complain to the Hungarian supervisory authority, the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), naih.hu, or to the authority where you live or work.

8 · Is it required?

Giving us your name, email, country, design title and description is necessary to make a submission — without them we cannot review anything or reply to you. The file, the portfolio link and marketing consent are optional. Declining marketing has no effect on your submission.

9 · Changes

If we change this notice we will publish the new version here with a new version number. The version shown to you at the time you submitted is recorded against your submission.
