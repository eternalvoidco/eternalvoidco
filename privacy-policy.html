Privacy Policy

Email Privacy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VOID© handles personal information submitted through newsletter and pre-order signup forms on eternalvoid.co.

Information we collect

When you sign up, we may collect your email address. For pre-order access, we may also collect your country or the country you type manually when selecting Other.

How we use your information

We use this information to send newsletter confirmations, pre-order access confirmations, drop updates, shipping availability updates, early release details, and brand communications related to VOID©.

Email delivery providers

We use trusted third-party services to operate the website and send emails, including Vercel for hosting and serverless functions, Resend for transactional email delivery, and Cloudflare for domain and email routing services.

Legal basis

We process signup information based on your consent when you submit a newsletter or pre-order form. You can withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us.

Data retention

We keep signup information only as long as needed to provide updates, manage pre-order access, and maintain our email communications. You can request deletion at any time.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information. You may also ask us to stop sending marketing or pre-order emails.

Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe from VOID© newsletter and pre-order email communications at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails or by visiting unsubscribe.html.

How to contact us

For privacy requests, email us at support@eternalvoid.co.

Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
