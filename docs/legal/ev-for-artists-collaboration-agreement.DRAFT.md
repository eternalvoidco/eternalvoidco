## 1 · Licence

1.1 The Artist grants ETERNAL VOID a licence to use the Design solely to develop, manufacture,
market, offer and sell the products in Schedule 1, through the channels in §3, in the territory in
§4, for the term in §5.

1.2 The licence is **non-exclusive** unless Schedule 2 expressly records exclusivity, its scope and
its duration. Silence is never exclusivity.

1.3 **The Artist retains ownership** of the Design and of all intellectual property in it. Nothing
here is an assignment. The Artist's moral rights are unaffected.

1.4 ETERNAL VOID may sub-license only to the manufacturers and service providers named in §7, only
to the extent needed to make and deliver the products, and only under equivalent confidentiality
and use restrictions.

1.5 The licence does **not** permit use of the Design to train, fine-tune or evaluate AI or
machine-learning systems, by ETERNAL VOID or by anyone it engages.

## 2 · Product categories

2.1 Limited to the categories in Schedule 1. Any other category requires a written amendment.

## 3 · Channels

3.1 Permitted at signature: **direct sales through eternalvoid.co**.

3.2 Wholesale, bundles, marketplaces and any third-party retail are **not permitted** until the
Parties have agreed in writing the royalty base for that channel, the rate, and the reporting
treatment. *(Drafting note: the royalty base defined in §8 is written for direct sales. Wholesale
is sold at a different price to a different buyer and needs its own base — most likely net invoiced
wholesale price — or the artist is paid on the wrong number.)*

## 6 · Approvals and design changes

6.1 ETERNAL VOID may make technical adaptations needed for production — repositioning, resizing,

6.2 A **material change** to the Design requires the Artist's prior written approval. Material
means a change that alters the Design's artistic substance. Approval is not to be unreasonably
withheld, and is deemed given if not refused within ____ business days of a written request.

6.3 The Artist receives ____ production samples free of charge.

6.4 ETERNAL VOID is not obliged to release the products. If it does not release by
____________________, the Artist may terminate under §14 and the licence ends.

## 7 · Manufacturer access

make the products, under written confidentiality and use restrictions no weaker than this

7.2 On request, ETERNAL VOID will tell the Artist which manufacturers hold the Design.

## 8 · Royalty

> **Drafting note, 2026-11-21 — not agreement text.** The public offer changed: the proposed royalty is now 12% of the **net profit** from sales of the covered products, and only a selected designer who provides a complete, production-ready tech pack qualifies for it. This section still defines **Net Product Sales** and states that costs are not deducted (§8.4), and §11 still promises a separate development fee for a production-ready tech pack. Both contradict the public offer. Counsel and the owner must redraft §8 around a defined net-profit calculation and decide what, if anything, §11 still pays for, before this agreement is used.

8.1 ETERNAL VOID pays the Artist **____ %** of Net Product Sales of the products in Schedule 1.

> *The public programme page proposes **12%** as ETERNAL VOID's opening offer. It is a proposal,
> not a benchmark, and it is pending owner approval. Whatever rate is signed here is also the rate
> that must be written into `artist_collaborations.royalty_bps`, because the royalty export reads
> that column and nothing else.*

8.2 **Net Product Sales** means, for direct sales, the amount actually paid by the customer for the
collaboration merchandise, after discounts, **excluding**:

   (a) VAT and other sales taxes; and
   (b) shipping charged separately to the customer.

8.3 **Reversals.** Refunds and chargebacks reverse the affected merchandise amount **once**,
including partial reversals. A reversal is applied in the quarter in which it occurs, which may be
later than the quarter of the sale.

8.4 **No other deductions.** Production, sampling, packaging, platform fees, payment processing
fees, marketing, fulfilment and overhead are **not** deducted from Net Product Sales. ETERNAL VOID
bears these when it runs the release.

8.5 **Worked example.** An eligible sale generating **€160** in Net Product Sales pays the Artist
**€19.20** at 12%.

8.6 Where a product in Schedule 1 is sold as part of a bundle with non-collaboration items, the
royalty is calculated on the collaboration item's share of the bundle price, allocated pro rata to
list price. *(Only applies once bundles are permitted under §3.2.)*

## 9 · Statements and payment

9.1 Within **30 days of the end of each calendar quarter**, ETERNAL VOID provides an itemised
statement showing, per SKU: eligible units, gross merchandise, discounts applied, reversals, the
royalty base, the rate, and the amount due.

9.2 Payment within the same 30 days, in EUR, to the account the Artist nominates.

9.3 Amounts under €____ carry forward to the next statement.

9.4 Where reversals in a quarter exceed Net Product Sales in that quarter, the excess carries
forward against the next quarter. No negative payment is ever raised.

9.5 The Artist is responsible for their own taxes. ETERNAL VOID will apply any withholding it is
legally obliged to apply and will provide a certificate.
*(Accounting to confirm before launch: invoicing direction — self-billing or artist invoice — EU
and non-EU VAT treatment, currency conversion method and date, and withholding by jurisdiction.)*

## 10 · Records and review

10.1 ETERNAL VOID keeps accurate records for ____ years.

10.2 The Artist may, once in any twelve months, on 30 days' notice, have an independent accountant
review those records at the Artist's cost, under confidentiality.

10.3 If a review shows an underpayment above **5%** for the reviewed period, ETERNAL VOID pays the
shortfall and the reasonable cost of the review.

## 11 · Development fees

11.1 If ETERNAL VOID asks the Artist, after selection, for a production-ready tech pack, additional
artwork, or substantial revisions, the Parties agree a **written scope and fee before that work
starts**.

11.2 Development fees are **not** advances against royalties and are **not** recoupable, unless
expressly stated otherwise in the written scope.

11.3 Development fees are payable whether or not the products reach release.

## 12 · Attribution

12.1 ETERNAL VOID credits the Artist under the credited name and in the placements in Schedule 1.

12.2 Neither Party announces the collaboration before the agreed announcement date.

12.3 Each Party may refer to the collaboration in its own portfolio after release, consistent with
Schedule 1.

## 13 · Restocks, inventory and sell-off

13.1 Restocks of a Schedule 1 SKU are permitted and earn royalty on the same terms.

13.2 New colourways and new SKUs require a **written amendment** adding them to Schedule 1.

13.3 On expiry or termination other than for ETERNAL VOID's material breach, ETERNAL VOID may sell
remaining inventory for **____ months**, with royalty payable as usual, and may thereafter dispose
of unsold inventory in a way that does not damage either Party's reputation. *(Confirm whether
donation or destruction is preferred.)*

## 14 · Termination

14.1 Either Party may terminate for the other's material breach not cured within 30 days of written
notice.

14.2 ETERNAL VOID may cancel the project before first production run, on written notice. Any
development fee already earned under §11 remains payable; no royalty arises.

14.3 The Artist may terminate if ETERNAL VOID has not released by the §6.4 date.

## 15 · After termination

15.1 The licence ends, subject to the §13.3 sell-off.

15.2 ETERNAL VOID stops making the products and withdraws the Design from forthcoming materials.
Historical campaign material and archive references may remain.

15.3 Royalties accrued before termination remain payable and the §9 statements continue until paid.

15.4 Confidentiality, records and review survive.

## 16 · Warranties and law

16.1 The Artist repeats the warranties given in the Submission Terms, and warrants that the Design
is original, that the Artist owns or controls the rights licensed here, and that any co-authors have
consented to this Agreement.

16.2 Each Party indemnifies the other against third-party claims arising from its own breach.
*(Counsel: confirm the cap and whether it should be mutual.)*

16.3 Governed by Hungarian law. Disputes subject to the courts of ____________________.

16.4 Nothing limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including under mandatory author
protections in the Hungarian Copyright Act (1999. évi LXXVI. törvény).
